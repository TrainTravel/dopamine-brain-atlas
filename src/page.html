Data and limits

Peaks and study metadata come from the open Neurosynth v7 database (14,371 fMRI papers, 507,891 peaks). A paper joins a layer when its abstract uses that layer's terms (tf-idf above 0.001), so a peak may come from patients, controls or a group contrast in either direction. Region centres are approximate MNI coordinates. Receptor densities are qualitative ratings (0 to 3) summarising autoradiography, PET and mRNA reports. Pathway curves are schematic. Use this for exploration and hypothesis generation, not for diagnosis.
